The Boteco Experience

Founded in 2015, Boteco is India's first and only authentic Brazilian restaurant chain, bringing the bold flavors, vibrant spirit, and festive soul of Brazil to the Indian dining scene. From the moment guests step inside, they're welcomed into a tropical haven filled with lush plants, handcrafted mosaic murals, and a lively, music-filled atmosphere inspired by the colorful streets of Rio de Janeiro. Every design detail is crafted to transport you to the heart of South America, making each visit feel like a celebration where cultures connect and stories are shared.
